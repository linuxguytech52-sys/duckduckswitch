Google → DuckDuckGo Redirector

When enabled, Google searches will automatically redirect to DuckDuckGo.

HTML version is more lightweight and privacy-focused. Regular version has more features.

Also redirect image searches from Google to DuckDuckGo.

Also redirect news searches from Google to DuckDuckGo.

Note: Google redirects are handled at the browser level using Chrome's Declarative Net Request API. Changes take effect immediately.
